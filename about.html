About Me

Online Trainer & Lifestyle Coach

I'm Jon, founder of SOVRYX Training, an online trainer and lifestyle coach. I served in the Marines for five years. I train hard each and every day. I coach the same way I train: no shortcuts, no fluff, just real work and real accountability.

I'm trying to help men and women from all walks of life reach their goals and improve their quality of life.

SOVRYX comes from "sovereign." It's about taking ownership of your own strength and discipline, and having someone in your corner while you do it.
